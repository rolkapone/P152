Design system v0.2

Deskfit (provisional name, P152)

Clean · Airy · Precise. Neutrals plus one accent, generous radii, soft layered shadows, glass panels floating over a studio-lit 3D scene. English UI, WCAG 2.2 AA, desktop first (min 1366×768).

Theme cycles System / Light / Dark (default: System). "Glass fallback" simulates the opaque fallback used when backdrop-filter is unsupported or prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce. To check reduced motion, enable "Reduce motion" in your OS.

4. Glass (panels over the 3D scene)

Recipe: tint at 86 %, blur(24px) saturate(180%), 1 px light border, layered shadow with a 1 px inner highlight. The tint is high enough that text stays at 4.5:1 or more even on the worst possible background (pure black under light glass, pure white under dark glass). Below: the same panel on four hard backgrounds, in light glass, dark glass, and the opaque fallback.

10. Motion

Smooth, short and never blocking. A light spring (about 3 % overshoot) is used for things that appear. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, every duration token is 0 ms and all animations and transitions are neutralized (camera jumps to the target view, loading is a static bar).
