Sprint 2 / design system v0.4

Native, premium, precise

The app is designed like a native desktop and tablet app: a unified toolbar, a source-list sidebar, a grouped-form inspector, and the 3D scene as a full-window canvas under translucent materials. Everything below uses only the tokens of tokens.css. Switch theme and the two accessibility options above; the frames adapt. No network, no external font: Inter is used if installed, otherwise the system font (the real app self-hosts Inter).

1. Typography

Inter with a scale modelled on the dynamic type styles (Large Title to Caption), optical tracking per size (Inter has no optical-size axis, so the letter-spacing does that job) and OpenType features. The minimum stays 14 px (WCAG-friendly, project rule): Footnote and Caption therefore share 14 px and differ by weight.

OpenType set:
Alt gives a single-storey "a" and open digits: friendlier, less neutral. Default recommended.

2. Colour

System-gray neutrals, one accent (system blue), semantic colours only for status. Hex values are read live from the tokens. The accent has two roles: --color-accent (text, rings, selection) and --color-accent-solid (filled buttons and switches with white text), which differ only in dark mode so both stay AA.

3. Materials (vibrancy)

Four levels of translucency, each with blur and saturation and a 1 px inner light hairline plus a 0.5 px outer outline. Contrast is computed on the worst background (pure black under light materials, pure white under dark ones), shown here as a hard stripe pattern. Rule of use: ultra-thin = glyphs only; thin = primary text and icons; regular = all text; thick = menus, popovers, sheets.

With "Reduce transparency" on (or prefers-reduced-transparency, or no backdrop-filter) every material becomes opaque, nothing else changes.

4. Shape and depth

Concentric corners: inner radius = outer radius minus the gap (22 - 8 = 14, 14 - 4 = 10, 10 - 4 = 6). Shadows are soft and layered; a 0.5 px hairline replaces hard borders.

5. Controls and lists

Same markup in both themes. Pill buttons (filled, tinted, plain), spring segmented control, switch, bevelled fields (the 1 px border is kept for the 3:1 non-text contrast), NumberField with stepper, grouped inspector lists, source-list rows with thumbnails and accent tint selection.

6. Overlays

Menus and popovers use the thick material; sheets are opaque with a blurred scrim and a spring entrance. Tooltips appear after 400 ms on hover and immediately on keyboard focus (Esc closes). Hover the buttons below.

7. Motion

Real spring curves (damped-spring equation exported as linear(), with a cubic-bezier fallback). --ease-spring overshoots about 4 %; --ease-spring-soft settles without overshoot (panels, sheets). Reduced motion removes all durations.

900 ms demo for comparison; in the app the spring token duration is 460 ms.

8. Icons

Lucide (ISC), stroke 1.6 px (range 1.5 to 1.75 to get closer to a system symbol look), sizes tied to text: 16 (14 px text), 18 (16 px text), 20 (toolbar), 24 (empty states). Inline paths here; the app uses the lucide-react homonyms.

9. Contrast audit (computed live, WCAG 2.2 AA)

The page reads the tokens and computes every ratio, including materials over the worst background and the hover fill. A red cell means a token regression.
