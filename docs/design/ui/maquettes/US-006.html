Choose your desk

The Desk tab of the sidebar: a grouped radio list of 7 desk models (5 rectangular, 2 corner) with a top-plan thumbnail at a common scale, an accent-tint selected row with a check, and a "Selected" summary group. The frames below show the sidebar and the scene only (the toolbar and inspector are in US-005). The scene drawing is a placeholder; its desk follows your choice at the real common scale.

6. For the developer

  • Components: RadioGroup "Desk model" (one Tab stop, arrows move and apply, Home / End, Space); row = Radio styled as .grp > .r.opt with Thumb (SVG from width, depth, shape, arm at the common scale, scale computed from the largest catalog model), text, Check (Lucide check). Group = --color-surface, --radius-lg, overflow: hidden. Section title Headline + help Subheadline (see markup in this page).
  • Selection is applied immediately (no Apply button, no confirmation); re-clicking the active row does nothing and announces nothing. The announcement is debounced 400 ms: "{name} selected. {w} by {d} cm."; one history entry per burst (US-019).
  • Catalog error: banner (error-bg, alert icon, Headline "Desks couldn't load", text, small default button "Reload page", role="alert") replaces the list and the summary.
  • Camera: after a change, the whole desk must fit in the free zone between the panes with a margin of at least 24 px (806 px wide at 1366 px, docked panes), 480 ms --ease-in-out, instant under reduced motion; orientation and Top / Front are kept.
  • Tokens used (all existing or new in v0.4): --radius-lg, --row-min-h, --thumb-w/h, --color-surface, --color-separator, --color-fill*, --color-accent-subtle(-text), --icon-stroke, --focus-*, .material-regular.
  • Contrast: dimensions on accent-subtle 6.7:1 (light) / 8.1:1 (dark); name (muted) on accent-subtle 7.0 / 7.5; muted on white 8.4 / on hovered row over material 4.5 (worst background); ring 3 px vs surface 5.2 / 8.0; thumbnail contour vs surface 8.4 and vs accent-subtle 6.7.
